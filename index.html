About

Morón, Buenos Aires - Argentina.

Gendema makes music and design the same way — alone, in one sitting, with no vote and no audience until it's finished. What doesn't survive that room doesn't get released.

Order isn't a limitation to him; it's the closest thing he has to a creed — gut and structure before explanation. Faith and precision run under most of what he makes, because one creates as one lives. He'd rather the work answer for him than the other way around.
